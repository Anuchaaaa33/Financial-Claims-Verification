# Financial-Claims-Verification
fact checked 10 popular financial claims related to relating to mutual funds, credit cards, taxation, insurance and investment products using regulatory publications and primary sources

#the 10 claims were
1. A mutual fund that performed best over the last 3 years is a good investment for the next 3 years.
2. Direct mutual funds always generate higher returns than regular mutual funds.
3. SIPs always reduce investment risk and guarantee better returns
4. A mutual fund investment is automatically diversified.
5. A zero-cost EMI means you pay no additional cost.
6. Prepaying a home loan is always better than investing the surplus money.
7. All mutual fund gains are taxed at the same rate.
8. Having health insurance through your employer is sufficient.
9. A higher sum insured always means better health insurance.
10. An investment offering 2–3% monthly returns with low risk is a good opportunity.
